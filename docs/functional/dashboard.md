# 2. Dashboard Module

## Purpose

Acts as the central landing page after authentication.

---

## Dashboard Cards

Display:

- Total Companies
- Total Documents
- Expiring Licenses
- Active Employees
- Recent Uploads

---

## Company List

Each company card displays:

- Company Logo
- Company Name
- Registration Number
- Status
- Last Updated
- Quick Actions

---

## Quick Actions

- Open
- Edit
- Delete
- Upload Document
- View Documents

---

## Search

Supports:

- Company Name
- Registration Number
- Email

---

## Filters

- Active
- Inactive
- Expired License
- Recently Added
